Features游戏特色

  • Dawn to night

    The sky turns from dawn to day, sunset and night every 10 points, and a lighthouse glows after dark.

  • Medals and best score

    Bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals. Your best score is saved on your device.

  • Music made in code

    A looping chiptune and seven sound effects, all synthesised live in your browser. One tap mutes everything.

  • Phone, tablet, desktop

    Tap, click or use the keyboard. The stage fits any screen, from small phones to ultra-wide monitors.

  • English and 中文

    Follows your browser language and remembers your choice. Switch any time.

  • Private and free

    No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is loaded except the game's own files.

How to play玩法

  1. Flap

    Tap the screen, click, or press Space, ↑ or W. Each flap lifts the puffin a little.

  2. Fly through the gaps

    Pass between the sea stacks for a point. Don't touch the rocks or the sea.

  3. Beat your best

    Score 10, 20, 30 and 50 for medals. It gets a little faster as you go.
